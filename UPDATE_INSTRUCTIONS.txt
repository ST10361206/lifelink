LIFELINK - WEBSITE DESIGN UPDATE

1. Stop editing files in Visual Studio 2022 for a moment.
2. Make a backup copy of your current C:\xampp\htdocs\lifelink folder.
3. Extract the downloaded LifeLink_website_updated.zip.
4. Open the extracted lifelink folder. Copy its CONTENTS (all files and folders).
5. Paste them into C:\xampp\htdocs\lifelink and choose REPLACE when Windows asks.
   Do not place a second lifelink folder inside the existing lifelink folder.
6. Open Visual Studio 2022 > File > Open > Folder > C:\xampp\htdocs\lifelink.
7. Start Apache and MySQL in XAMPP.
8. In your browser, visit http://127.0.0.1/lifelink/ and press Ctrl+F5 to refresh CSS.
9. Test Home, About Us, Donation Centres, Contact, Login, and Register.

IMPORTANT
- No database import is required for this design update.
- Existing login, registration, appointments and dashboard files are preserved.
- Donation sites on the public Centres page are real published locations, but they
  are not connected to the LifeLink appointment database. Verify before visiting.
- Contact form opens an email draft with no recipient; the project team must add
  its own address later. It does not save or send contact messages.
- Photos are loaded from Unsplash and require internet access.
- This is a student prototype, not a live medical service.
